What we don't do — and why.

Most prep platforms tell you everything they do. This page does the opposite. Counter-positioning by transparency: the clearest way to know if we're a match is to read, before you sign up, exactly where our edges are. If one of these is a dealbreaker for you — good. You just saved yourself two hours.

6 explicit limits Static — no fine print, no asterisks Published before signup, not after
01

No classroom replacement

ScoreForge is digital-native. We don't teach the GMAT or GRE from scratch, and we won't pretend a dashboard replaces a good teacher. If you need live instruction from zero, that's a real need — just not ours, and we'll point you somewhere honest instead of pretending otherwise.

Where to learn the fundamentals — channels & courses we rate
02

No 1:1 mentorship at scale

Our deep mock analysis is the analytical work a mentor would otherwise do by hand — delivered on every mock, at scale. But the relational half of mentorship — accountability, a human who knows your story — is intentionally outside what we automate. We won't fake it with a chatbot.

Mentor-pairing — opt-in, planned for V2
03

No temperament installation

Exam-day composure is largely outside a platform's reach. We can measure your patterns under pressure — where fatigue lands, where guessing creeps in — and flag them honestly. We can't install calm. We monitor and surface; we don't claim to transform who you become on test day.

04

No score guarantees

Outcome guarantees are marketing artifacts. A single-sitting exam carries real variance no honest provider can promise away. We guarantee the quality of the analysis and the honesty of the diagnosis — never a number on a screen. Anyone who guarantees the number is selling you the variance.

05

No essays, interviews or app consulting — in this module

Essays, recommendations, school lists, and interviews are their own craft. This module is test prep. Mastery includes light readiness drills, not done-for-you consulting — and we say so plainly rather than blurring the line to look fuller than we are.

Admissions module — committed for V2
06

No other entrance exams

We don't cover the SAT, LSAT, the Executive Assessment, or board exams. Focus is the product: we'd rather do GMAT and GRE properly than do every test passably. If your exam isn't here, we'll tell you on day one instead of upselling you something half-built.

Where we might be wrong

The limits we're least sure about.

Counter-positioning cuts both ways. Here's where our own confidence is lowest — published, not buried in a footnote.

A

Our selection model is trained on a beta cohort. Small-sample bias is real; we recalibrate openly as the data grows, and we'll show our work.

B

Program-weighting estimates are directional, not official admissions math. Treat them as honest hypotheses, not promises from a committee.

C

We may be overstating how much "selection" alone moves a stubborn score for some candidates. Where it doesn't, we'll say that to your face.

When we find we're wrong, we change the product and tell you what changed. That commitment is the entire point of this page. Read our open risks & questions
